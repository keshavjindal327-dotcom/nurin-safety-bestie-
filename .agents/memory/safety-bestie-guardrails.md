---
name: Safety Bestie product guardrails
description: Product maturity and privacy constraints for NURIN's Safety Bestie concept.
---

Safety Bestie is a proposed personal-safety keychain concept in development; it has not been manufactured. SOS activation, emergency-contact messaging, and location sharing are proposed features that have not been validated. The early-access list is only an expression of interest, not a purchase or preorder. Do not promise protection, emergency response, availability, or functionality. Signup information must not be sold or displayed publicly.

**Why:** The user explicitly described the product as unmanufactured and unvalidated, and set privacy boundaries for submitted information.

**How to apply:** Keep these disclosures clear in marketing, FAQ, form, and future product copy; do not add checkout or public signup data.
